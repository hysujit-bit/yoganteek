Are You Under Stress? Know How to Manage It

Published May 2025 · By Dr. Jayashree Pattanayak

Stress is a mental or emotional state that results from adverse circumstances. It's a kind of psychological pain. It can be of two kinds:

  • Positive stress that helps to improve performance or helps to evolve the person's state.
  • Negative stress that is a sign of a disturbed or depressed mind. Negative stress impacts our entire body, mind, and spirit.

Pathophysiology of Stress

This is a major cause behind acid reflux. In our physical body it is required to maintain a balance between acid and alkaline to keep the body perfectly healthy and hence mostly it regulates the mood and emotions. So our body should not be more acidic or highly alkaline.

Sometimes apart from the diet, our negative mental stress acts as an acid booster in the body. In the other way, a disturbed or depressed mind is an indication of irregular function of physique.

Stress Relieving Techniques

  • Get some quality time for self.
  • Take the situation as it is and respond before you react.
  • Yoga and relaxation.

Yogic Management of Stress

Yoga is a complete lifestyle that includes physical practices, breathing practices and relaxation, meditation. Yoga is a total compilation of asanas, pranayama, diet and meditation.

Asanas

Asanas are to stretch the body, allowing the prana to flow more. Remaining in a posture makes the mind more stable enabling the breath flow slow and steady, adding on strength and stability to the muscles and bones.

Pranayama

Pranayama is a control over breath and modification of the flow. It lifts the mind to the deeper state of calmness which brings us to a meditative state. The more deep and long is the breath, so the person has a strong and right decision making power.

Diet

Pure and proper diet plays a great role for the mind. Sattvic diet is easy to digest, it is nutritious, never exaggerates the body mechanism and indirectly helps for a lite and better mood, reducing the stress.

Meditation

Meditation acts as a capsule for any kind of mental and emotional disturbances. It has the power to take the mind straight away from the negative and complexities of life towards peace and positivity.

Through regular practice of yoga and meditation, one can effectively manage stress and lead a more balanced, peaceful life.
