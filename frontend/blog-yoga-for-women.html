Yoga for Women: A Complete Guide

Published Mar 2025 · By Dr. Jayashree Pattanayak

These days we see young girls, ladies running along with the fast moving time. Every single person is efficiently working to prove themselves regardless of the age and relationship. But along with the hectic schedule, our women are not able to take care of themselves physically, emotionally and hence get affected by many severe hormonal issues or emotional blockage or psychophysical issues.

In a home, a woman spends her life as a homemaker regardless of the price and time for her kids, husband and other dear ones. But fails to spend some quality time for self. As a result of which suffers from so many common usual pains like back pain, neck pain, sciatica nerve pain, arthritis, PCOD etc.

The Modern Challenge

Girls who are too ambitious and fix their goals in jobs spend maximum time for study and once get the job they run after proving themselves as they are not less than anybody else. I am not against the job or earning money or leading a better respectable life, but in the mid our lovely ladies forget to boost their required immunity, to look after their hormones, give some pace for their mental stability. That brings a major problem for their health and mind.

This drastic change in their lifestyle and food habit is making them more strong and confident in outlook but more or less it is making them more weak, tired, unhealthy, emotional irritable as a whole an unstable character pop up.

We know today's girl is tomorrow's mother. Mother is the only way to give birth to a healthy and a genius future of a baby. Apart from that to maintain a good and healthy physique, a boost in immunity, a balanced mind yoga helps as a friend of a lady.

Benefits of Yoga for Women

Yoga acts as a body and mind cleanser by lifting the energy level and self-esteem to a great level. A little practice of yoga can go a long way in managing many chronic medical conditions:

  • Yoga helps lower blood pressure, cholesterol and reduces the risk of heart disease.
  • It has a great role in pain management – back pain, joint pain.
  • It helps reducing stress.
  • It helps to cope with depression naturally.
  • It helps in many conditions like insomnia, depression.
  • It reduces overweight, maintains the BMI and tones the body.
  • It improves sleep by making brain calm.

Start Your Journey

JSP Yoga group tries to invoke the real power within you through yoga. We need to bring out a minimum time slot for the practice of yoga to keep ourselves more rejuvenated and to bring a sync in our thought and work. Instead of taking multivitamin capsules and other medicines regularly for our health and fitness it is always better to improve our diet and add some guided exercises along with relaxation to our daily routine.
