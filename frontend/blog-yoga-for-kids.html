Yoga for Kids: Building Strong Bodies and Minds

Published Apr 2025 · By Dr. Jayashree Pattanayak

It's hard to be a kid now-a-days. Our children live in a super-fast world of busy schedules of parents, dealing with many distractions, overstimulation, challenging school life, improper diet, video games (mobile/tabs), malls, and computers. We usually don't think this can be stressful for our kids rather than relaxation.

JSP Yoga has found that yoga is a low-cost, helpful tool which can help to overcome these pressures. When our children will learn the yogic procedure for good health, mental relaxation, and inner joy, they can fight with life's challenges with a little more ease and fundamentally.

Why Yoga for Kids?

Yoga at an early age encounters self-esteem and motivates body fitness with a psychophysical activity. Yogis have developed the asanas as representatives of the natural world and used animals and plants for inspiration.

When children imitate the qualities of nature, they have a chance to live like them taking on its qualities. For example, the lion (Simhasana) pose makes our kids learn not only the power of the lion, but also their own sense of power: like when to be aggressive and when to retreat.

Yoga with children provides many opportunities to explore wisdom, share quality times, and to create the foundation for a lifelong practice that will continue to deepen.

Benefits of Yoga for Kids

Children get enormous benefits from yoga. Physically, it enhances their strength, flexibility, concentration, growth and mental awareness. In addition, their state of thinking and sense of behaviour improves.

Yoga is the key source of relaxation for children. With JSP Yoga, children can be connected more deeply with the inner self, and develop a friendly relationship with the nature that surrounds them.

Yoga helps kids to:

  1. Strengthen growing bodies and maintain flexibility
  2. Boost concentration
  3. Build self-esteem
  4. Improve body awareness
  5. Provide a peaceful, relaxed state of mind and body
  6. Serve as best tools for stress management
  7. Spark natural creativity in ripe imaginations
  8. Explore social interactions
  9. Enhance psychophysical awareness
  10. Cultivate discipline and responsibility

What JSP Yoga Has Designed for Kids

Breathing Exercises/Awareness

Breathing exercises can stimulate kids or provide relaxation. Different asanas and techniques help kids to connect with the flow of breathing. Breathing exercises increase lung capacity and provide better immunity. Hormonal secretion can be harmonised through these kinds of practices.
