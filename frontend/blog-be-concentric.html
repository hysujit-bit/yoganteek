Be Concentric: The Key to Success

Published Jun 2025 · By Dr. Jayashree Pattanayak

A great Holy Man said, the only way to success is "Be concentric." It is the secret path to achieve everything.

Everyone dreams of being a great person, a successful person. But a person can be said as successful if one has 4 balanced aspects in life: a balanced healthy personal life, professional life, family life, and social life. If any one aspect gets disturbed and although it has a very big name and fame or a very good bank balance, still he/she cannot be said as a successful person.

How Can It Be Possible?

Varieties of suggestions may come:

  • Plan out your goal.
  • Seize your points of distraction.
  • Focus on your goal and keep trying to move in the path overcoming all obstacles.
  • Try to imagine and visualize your dream.

These are beautiful ways to take you to your dream, but most of the time these are one-way solutions.

Sometime we find it very difficult when we focus on our career, we make our family life disturbed, or we compromise with our personal health and mind. That is how by the time we earn something, we lose many things at the other side.

The Concentric Approach

But if we learn the trick to be concentric, it becomes easier to get and maintain the success and lead a better life.

While the word concentration comes, we visualize something like trataka — sitting and gazing at a point or doing some yoga postures to improve our concentration and cognitive function. Yes, of course these work, but apart from that we'll discuss something else today.

We all love to be loved by everyone, to be praised by everyone and for that we try a lot too but always remain unsatisfied. The real satisfaction comes when we love someone or something wholeheartedly and get the same in return.

Most important and secret psychology of human being is when we love someone we try to become like him and we try to follow him without any doubt and complain. There inadvertently we nurture our love centre and get the spark to move ahead, to reach the ultimate.

Here comes a balance in life which we call as real yoga.

So, higher and broader the centre of love, the ideal, the better. For, as high and broad our Ideal is, so high and broad we too become. Only we need to take to the habit of a creeper and entwine around the tree of the ideal; fulfilment of desire shall be ours.

There comes the concentration automatically. We get the energy, the direction, the guidance, the knowledge to solve the problems and to overcome the obstacles physically, mentally, and spiritually.

This is the only easiest way to become a successful person and to maintain a great successful life.
